## Editorial direction

The recommended hero line is **“Şehirden bir tekne uzağa.”** It belongs to this event because the crossing is not a logistical footnote; it is how the day begins. The copy below assumes that a reference is optional and that the application exists to manage a fifty-person format, not to judge whether somebody is “the right kind of person.” Confirm that assumption before publishing.

Avoid these words unless they describe something provable: *eşsiz, unutulmaz, büyülü, dönüştürücü, ilham verici, ruhunu besleyen, benzer düşünen insanlar*. The images already provide atmosphere. The words should provide character and clarity.

### What this day is

**Eyebrow**
What kind of day?

**Headline**
The programme is set. The day is yours.

**Body**
This is not a festival you watch from the edge. Sit down for a workshop, leave halfway for a swim, add to a painting someone else began, or disappear into the back garden for a quiet half-hour. Do not try to complete everything. Start wherever your curiosity takes you.

**Three short principles**

- **Take part.** Make something, try something, make a sound, get paint on your hands.
- **Meet.** Board the same boat, sit at the same table, leave room for an unexpected conversation.
- **Move at your own pace.** There is a programme, but no compulsory route through it.

### Day flow

**Eyebrow**
The day

**Headline**
We leave Istanbul at noon. We return after midnight.

**Intro**
The hours in between are not tied to one stage or one running order. The day changes direction three times; you choose your route within it.

**01 · It starts on the boat**
Our private boat leaves Karaköy at 12:30 and Kadıköy at 13:00. The first set begins as the city falls behind us, and we arrive directly at the house pier.

**02 · We spread through the garden**
Workshops, open studios, the pool and bar come alive across the grounds. Some sessions happen in small groups; others are spaces you can enter and leave in your own time.

**03 · We meet at the table**
As the sun reaches the water, we lay the long table. The music rises with the evening and turns into the night set. The return boat leaves the pier at 00:30.

#### Collective Sound

**Card line**
First we listen to the island. Then we play what we heard with voice, breath and rhythm.

**Detail**
What does Büyükada sound like? We will take a short sound walk around the house, listening to the wind, water, birds and one another. Then Dr Oğuz Öner will guide us through Soundpainting, weaving those sounds into a live, one-off composition. You do not need musical training—only a willingness to listen and make a sound.

**Meta**
15:00–16:15 · 75 minutes · 15 people · House and surroundings

#### Fragrance Lab

**Card line**
From scent memory to perfume composition, with your own bottle to take home.

**Detail**
With Şeyma Çavdur, we will look at how scent settles into memory, how the nose works and how a perfume is built. You will explore individual notes, map your own scent memory and compose a small fragrance of your own.

**Meta**
16:30–18:00 · 90 minutes · 10 people · Back garden

**Card line**
What do the colours, textures and pieces you choose without thinking say about you?
