## Final prompts

### Watercolor

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide website footer illustration, 3:1 landscape.
Primary request: Create an original Istanbul Büyükada waterfront illustration inspired by the first user reference's delicate architectural ink and watercolor panorama. The references are style guidance only, not edit targets. Depict Büyükada's historic ferry terminal, elegant white timber island mansions with terracotta roofs, pine-covered hill, waterfront promenade, and a traditional white Istanbul passenger ferry on pale turquoise Marmara water. Authentic intimate island scale. Fine hand-drawn architectural details and soft watercolor washes, restrained sage greens, cream, terracotta and sea blue. Compose a continuous low waterfront panorama across the width, with irregular softly painted water edge. Background solid warm cream #f7f5ef, no rectangular sky wash. Illustration fills most of image height with a little empty cream above the skyline. No text, typography, logos, UI, border, Indian monuments or other cities. High resolution.

### Paper collage

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide website footer illustration, 3:1 landscape.
Primary request: Create an original Istanbul Büyükada illustrated paper-cut travel collage inspired by the second user reference's colorful layered destination stickers. References are style guidance only, not edit targets. Entire subject is Büyükada: ornate white wooden island villas with terracotta roofs, historic ferry terminal, clock tower, pine trees, a classic white Istanbul ferry, bicycle, seagulls and curling turquoise Marmara waves. Arrange as a lush overlapping single horizontal panorama, varied height silhouette, tactile paper edges and subtle ivory sticker outlines. Joyful sophisticated travel illustration, rich forest and sage greens, teal water, warm ochre and coral accents. Background solid warm cream #f7f5ef, compact silhouette with minimal empty space above. Fill lower edge with layered waves and foliage. No text, typography, logos, UI, foreign monuments or mosque skyline. High resolution.
